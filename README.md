<h1>Blueberry Muffins</h1>

<h2>INGREDIENTS</h2>

<p>2 1/2 cups Wholesome Yum Blanched Almond Flour</p>
<p>1/2 cup Besti Monk Fruit Allulose Blend</p>
<p>1/2 tbsp Baking powder</p>
<p>1/4 tsp Sea salt</p>
<p>1/3 cup Unsalted butter</p>
<p>1/3 cup Unsweetened almond milk</p>
<p>3 large Eggs</p>
<p>1/2 tsp Vanilla extract</p>
<p>3/4 cup Blueberries</p>

<h2>RECIPE</h2>

<p>Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (177 degrees C). Line a muffin pan with 10 or 12 silicone or parchment paper muffin liners. (Use 12 for lower calories/carbs, or 10 for larger muffin tops.)</p>

<p>In a large bowl, stir together the almond flour, Besti, baking powder and sea salt.</p>

<p>Mix in the melted butter or coconut oil, almond milk, eggs, and vanilla extract. Fold in the blueberries.</p>

<p>Distribute the batter evenly among the muffin cups. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until the tops are golden and an inserted toothpick comes out clean.</p>
